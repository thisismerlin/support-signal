# Datasheet: Larkspur demo dataset

Format follows *Datasheets for Datasets* (Gebru et al.).

## Motivation
Built to demonstrate Support Signal. Larkspur is a fictional B2B company selling workforce scheduling software (Rota, Timesheets, Payroll Connect). Every flaw is planted deliberately so each verdict teaches something. Nothing is derived from any real company's data, taxonomy or distributions.

## Composition
- 900 fictional accounts; 875 raised at least one case.
- About 5,100 case rows over twelve months (1 Oct 2025 to 30 Sep 2026).
- Two exports of the same cases: `larkspur_snapshot.csv` (final state only) and `larkspur_with_history.csv` (adds reopen counts, owner and group counts, customer wait time), plus `larkspur_history_log.csv` (owner, group and status changes).
- Customer text is template-generated. Names are placeholders ("Agent K").

## Collection process
Generated by `generator/generate_demo.py` with fixed seed 20261001. Running it twice produces identical files.

## Planted flaws and expected verdicts

| Flaw | How it's planted | Expected verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duplicate rows | 0.5% of rows repeated | C4 amber |
| History starts late | Field history switched on 12 Jan 2026 | C3 amber (history export) |
| Catch-all reasons | About a quarter of cases logged "Other", "General" or blank | B2 red |
| Near-duplicate reasons | "Login issue" / "Login Issues", "SSO" / "Single sign-on" | B3 amber |
| Bad dates | About 1% of closed cases closed before they were created | B4 amber |
| No wait time in snapshot | Only the history export has customer wait time | B5 needs a human (snapshot), green (history) |
| Unlinked follow-ups | Reopen counts exist but follow-ups aren't linked | B6 amber (history export) |
| Ignored escalation flag | Flag set on a small fraction of cases that went to engineering | B7 needs a human (snapshot), red (history) |
| Team-type reasons | "Tier 2", "Escalated to Engineering", "Billing team" on about 13% of cases | AI2 amber |
| Thin resolution records | Resolution notes or articles on under half of closed cases | AI4 amber |
| Survey response bias | Quickly resolved cases are surveyed more often | Noted in B8 |

## The planted causal structure (for the driver panel)
- Churn depends on **product** (Payroll Connect churns most) and on one genuine service effect: an account having a case that was **slow (30+ days) and passed around (3+ owners)**.
- Case volume depends on product and account size, **not** on churn.
- So raw "drivers" correlated with case volume (10+ cases, phone contact, high priority, "Other" reasons) look predictive and fall away once product and case volume are controlled. The planted effect holds.
- With a dozen flags tested, the odd one will look protective by chance. That's left in deliberately.

## Uses
Demonstration and testing of Support Signal only. Not suitable for benchmarking real support operations.

## Maintenance
Regenerate with `npm run data`. Expected verdicts live in `data/expected.json` and are enforced by `npm test`.
