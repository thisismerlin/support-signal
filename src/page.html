Support Signal

draft · rules 0.2

Before you buy the AI, check what you're feeding it. Support Signal reads a support case export and tells you which questions it can honestly answer: resolution time, transfers, CSAT, churn and AI self-help.

Each check that passes switches on the signals that data can carry. Checks that fail keep their signals locked, with the reason and the fix. The demo below is Larkspur, a made-up scheduling software company with deliberately messy data.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.

    Why most churn drivers aren't

    Busier accounts raise more cases, so almost anything tied to case volume looks like a churn driver. Compare like with like and most of them fall away.

    Each row is a flag on an account. Dots further right mean higher odds of churning.
